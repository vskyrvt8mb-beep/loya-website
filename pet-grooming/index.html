Frequently asked questions

Does it work for a salon with a pet shop?

Yes. Track grooming visits and food purchases alike — with points rewards.

How does a client get a card?

From a link or QR code at the desk: the owner enters a name and phone number, and the card is saved to Google Wallet or Telegram.

What if a client has several pets?

Keep a separate card per pet or one shared card per owner — whichever suits you.
