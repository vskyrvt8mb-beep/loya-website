Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Download the app and click “Try 14 days free” — just an email, no card. Every feature works for 14 days. Afterwards subscribe on the website or stay on the free version (up to 10 customers) — your data stays.

Do I need to buy a scanner?

No. You can scan with the laptop camera or a staff member’s phone. A regular USB barcode scanner works too.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. The card arrives by email, in Telegram or in Google Wallet — the customer just shows the QR on their phone.

Where is customer data stored?

On your computer, not on someone else’s server. The app backs up automatically every day and works offline.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 computers (installer or Microsoft Store) and Android 7+ tablets and phones (an .apk file). One-click installation, automatic updates.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, in one click from the link in your confirmation email — no calls, no explanations.

Can I import customers from another app?

Yes — upload a CSV table (for example from Excel) and your customers appear in Loya.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Slovak, Russian and Ukrainian. Customer emails go out in each customer’s own language.
