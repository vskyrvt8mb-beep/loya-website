Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to install an app?

No. The card opens from a link or QR code and is saved to Google Wallet or Telegram. You can also print plastic cards with a QR code.

Do I need a special scanner?

No — any staff member’s phone works. A USB barcode scanner works too if you have one.

Does it work for a bakery or a small chain?

Yes. For several locations there’s the Pro plan: a shared database, per-location analytics, staff and roles.
