Frequently asked questions

Does it work for a photographer without a studio?

Yes. Loya runs on a laptop and the clients’ cards live on their phones — no studio required.

Can I use it for studio rentals?

Yes — record each rental as a visit or an amount, and regular renters earn a bonus.

Do clients need to install anything?

No. The card opens from a link or QR code and is saved to Google Wallet or Telegram.
