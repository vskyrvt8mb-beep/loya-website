Frequently asked questions

Does it work if clients come rarely?

Yes — that’s exactly what it’s for: the points card and campaigns keep clients between trips, and their bonus waits for the next holiday.

Do clients have to visit the office?

No. The card opens from a link or QR code and is saved to Google Wallet or Telegram; a purchase can be recorded by the card code.

Can I run several offices?

Yes — the Pro plan covers several locations: a shared database, staff and per-office analytics.
