Frequently asked questions

Does it work for a barbershop or nail studio?

Yes. Loya works for any service: haircuts, nails, cosmetics, massage, spa.

How do clients get a card?

From a link or a QR code at the front desk: the client enters a name and phone number, and the card is saved to Google Wallet or Telegram.

Yes — after a visit, a review request can be sent automatically.
